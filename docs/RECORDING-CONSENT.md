# Recording: off by default, and only with everyone's agreement

Recording is not built yet. This is how it will work (founder decision F11, ROADMAP 9.1 R-L1).

1. Recording is off by default, for every meeting.
2. `meet.start_recording` is a `confirm: human` tool. When an agent asks for it, a person must say yes first.
3. Before anything is recorded, everyone in the call sees a notice and hears a spoken one: who started it, and that they can leave or turn their camera and microphone off.
4. Each person's answer is stored (`recording_consents`: who, what they answered, when the notice was shown).
5. People who join while recording is on see the notice before their media is sent.
6. Recordings can be deleted by the host at any time, and are exported with everything else.

Several US states (for example California) need every party's consent, and the EU treats recordings as personal data. This is general knowledge, not legal advice.
